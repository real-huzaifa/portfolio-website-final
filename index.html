ABOUT

I'm a Data Science undergraduate at Pak-Austria Fachhochschule, working mainly in Python, SQL and Power BI. Most of my time goes into data preprocessing, visualisation and model training — turning messy datasets into something a decision-maker can actually act on.

What I care about is finishing things. A model in a notebook isn't a result; a model behind a live API is. That's why my fraud detection project ended in a containerised FastAPI service on a public cloud endpoint rather than a set of validation scores. I also pay attention to choosing the right metric — PR-AUC over accuracy on a 3.5% positive rate, recall over precision when the cost is a missed at-risk customer.

"A model in a notebook isn't a result. A model in production is." — How I pick what to work on

Across four internships I've worked on churn prediction, retail and transport analytics, A/B testing and SQL reporting, and I've built dashboards and written reports aimed squarely at non-technical stakeholders. Right now I'm deepening my work in machine learning and deployment, and I'm looking to contribute on a team where I can keep shipping.
